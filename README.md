### 👨‍💻 Professional Summary

I am a **Software Engineer**, **Data Scientist**, and **Systems Builder** with operational roots as an **Airport Rescue Firefighting (ARFF) Officer** at Hang Nadim International Airport (PT. Bandara Internasional Batam).

Aviation emergency response permits zero margin for error and demands calm, decisive execution under pressure. I channel this exact operational rigor into engineering **fault-tolerant backend architectures, resilient web systems, and automated agentic AI workflows**.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** High-throughput microservices in **Go**, systems engineering in **Rust**, and mission-critical fullstack platforms with **Next.js & TypeScript**.
- 🤖 **AI Workflows:** Engineering LLM-orchestrated autonomous agents, data pipelines, and production machine learning models.
- 📐 **Domain Expertise:** Civil aviation safety standards (ICAO Annex 14), operational ERP systems, and high-stakes command automation.
